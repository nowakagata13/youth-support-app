# YouthSupport

This is the demo version of the service for the young people that recommends lockdown compliant activities
that invoke positive feelings in young adults.

Current version of application can take data from potential customers together with their personality traits and activities which they were doing before lockdown. The application calculate the feelings of the teenager in lockdown and then it can suggest type of activities which the person can do based on their prefernces and personality. At the end, it shows feeling of the person with the suggested activities.
